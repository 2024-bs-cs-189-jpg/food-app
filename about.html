Our Story

A Kitchen Built On Honest Food

Savor & Co. began in 2014 as a single neighbourhood kitchen with one rule: cook the way you would for your own family. A decade later, that rule hasn't changed.

Our Mission

To make thoughtfully prepared, ingredient-led food accessible to every household — without compromising on flavour, freshness or care.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted everyday kitchen in every city we serve, known for consistency, warmth and genuine hospitality.

Milestones

Our Journey So Far

2014

The First Kitchen

Opened our very first location with a menu of just twelve dishes.

2017

Citywide Delivery Launch

Introduced our own delivery fleet to guarantee food arrives fresh and hot.

2021

Three New Locations

Expanded to three neighbourhood kitchens to shorten delivery times.

2026

50+ Dishes, One Standard

Now serving thousands of customers weekly, still cooking every dish to order.
